## Switching Fabric

The managed switch acts as:

- The central Layer 2 aggregation point
- The 802.1Q trunk termination point from the gateway
- The SPAN/mirror source for network monitoring
- The access layer for wired and wireless clients

All bare-metal systems connect directly to the switch.

## Monitoring Integration

Traffic visibility is achieved via:

- SPAN/mirror of trunk traffic to Security Onion
- Gateway log export from OpenWRT
- Host-level telemetry from infrastructure nodes

This ensures network-level observability without altering routing behavior.

## Design Constraints

- Single upstream WAN ingress
- Single internal switching fabric
- No distributed routing
- No unmanaged switching
- Centralized traffic observation

Logical segmentation and routing behavior are documented separately under VLAN Segmentation and Routing.
